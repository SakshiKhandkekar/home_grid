The Beauty of Nature

Nature gives us life, peace, and endless beauty. Let's protect it for a better tomorrow.

Everything we see around us constitutes nature, including the sun, the moon, trees, flowers, fruits, birds, and animals. Nature provides us with oxygen, food, water, shelter, and medicines. Every living creature depends on nature for survival. Nature is not just a place; it is our home.
